Golf Canada Alexa Skill

Login to give the Golf Canada Alexa skill the ability to perform user-centric requests:

  • Ask about your current handicap and round history,
  • Ask about your friends handicap and round history,
  • Save new rounds scorecards through conversation,
  • and whatever else might become available...

Information

Logging is not required for retrieving news and general information. For more details or issues please see Golf Canada Alexa website.
