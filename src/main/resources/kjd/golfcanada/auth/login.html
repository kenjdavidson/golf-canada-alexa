Golf Canada Alexa Skill

Login with your Golf Canada account in order to:

  • Ask about your current handicap and round history,
  • Ask about your friends handicap and round history,
  • Save new rounds scorecards through conversation,
  • and whatever else might become available...

Information

Golf Canada Alexa skill requires logging in with your Golf Canada account information in order to access your private scorecard, friends and course related details. Golf Canada Alexa website.
